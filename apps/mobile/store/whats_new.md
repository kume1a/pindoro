# What's new

What each version brings, newest first, for the people who install Pindoro. The iOS release
workflow puts the section of the version it builds into the SideStore source as its release notes
and fails when that section is missing, so write it with every version bump.

## 2.20.0

Tbilisi first
- New streets now stay inside the city: its main, secondary, tertiary and minor roads first, then the outskirts.
- The outskirts are Tskneti, Betania, Kojori, Shindisi, Tabakhmela, Tsavkisi, Okrokana, Gldani village, Zahesi, Lilo, the airport and Dighomi village, and the roads out to them and to the border.
- Outskirts streets you already learned wait until their turn. Your own routes keep going.

Streets that share a name
- When two streets have the same name, the question says which one: "Shota Rustaveli Street · Tskneti".
- The Tbilisi Bypass Road is one street now, not three.

Fixes
- Today shows Done once the day's work is done, also after the pointing drills unlock.
- Hiding a place you can't find no longer keeps the day from counting.
- A street you got right only slowly in Explore is no longer marked as known on the next launch.

## 2.19.1

Fixes
- After I don't know, the answer shows on its own: no half-placed pin, ends or exit left from before.
- Name it shows the street names and "Did you know it?" after I don't know.
- The both-ends question no longer shows a place named after the street.
- A second recital in a row starts with an empty field.
- A landmark said by its English name ("Vake Park") counts in a recital.
- A quick double tap on Got it or Next no longer skips the next street.
- Explore keeps an answer for your reviews even when you leave the round right after it.

Large and bold text
- The search field, the streak calendar, map labels and the week's bars grow with large text instead of clipping.
- Buttons and labels make room for bold text.

Also
- Lighter on memory, and a lot of tidying under the hood.

## 2.19.0

"I don't know" on every question
- Can't place a street or name a turn? Tap I don't know instead of guessing. It counts as a miss, shows you the answer and brings the street back soon.
- Works in lessons, reviews, route cards, recitals and Explore rounds.

The route preview shows every turn
- Step through all the turns of a route, not just the key ones, each turned so the way in points up with the same arrow Guided draws.
- Recite the turns covers them all too.

Clearer level picker
- Levels you can't pick yet show a lock; tap one to see why.

Georgian fits better
- Long Georgian words are never split across lines; buttons stack when they don't fit side by side.
- Turn instructions read naturally ("turn left onto …"), and the Georgian voice says metres and kilometres in full.

Also
- The next review date is no longer a day early.
- Explore keeps working after you leave a round midway.
- Today and Progress stay on screen while they refresh.
- Typing a street name in a recital or crossings drill keeps the keyboard open.
- Report this turn asks before it counts the turn as allowed.
- Screens opened during a night drive stay dark, and Guided by day keeps a readable status bar.

## 2.18.0

A smoother Guided map
- The car glides along the road instead of jumping once a second, and the map turns with the road ahead.
- While the map follows you, only the places along your route show; drag the map or open the routes to see them all.
- The turn arrow shows one turn at a time, even where the road jogs right and then straight away left.
- U-turns turn left, as traffic keeps right.

Better results after a drive
- It says how you found the way: on your own, with help at some turns, or led by the voice all the way.
- A planned U-turn no longer makes a drive look shorter than the best route.
- Arriving at a place says its name, and each turn of the debrief is framed around its junction.

Also
- The route chooser checks where you really are and plans again if you've moved.

## 2.17.0

Drive to places, not just streets
- Search every shop, café, pharmacy and landmark, or tap a place on the map for its address, opening hours, phone and website.
- Drop a pin anywhere and drive right there. Routes that stop on your side of the road come first.

Pick your route like a navigator
- Each way there is a card with its time and length, and a bubble on its line ("Same time", "+3 min").
- Pick one to light it up, then tap Let's go.

Stops on the way
- Add up to 5 stops before you leave, or while driving: a passenger can do it at any speed.
- Pindoro says when you reach each stop and carries on to the end.

A new look for Guided
- The map tilts like a real navigator, with raised buildings, house numbers, traffic lights and a bigger car.

Also
- The planning map's marks (your start, the flag, the route bubbles, the turn numbers) stay in place while you drag the map.
