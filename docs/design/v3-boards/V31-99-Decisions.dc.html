Closing note: one job per tab, one count, one header

Today says what to do now and what comes next. Explore is map-first free play with Play in the thumb zone. Progress shows how memory grows, where, and what’s coming. Me changes only where these decisions touch it.

1 · Today’s drive could show nothing to do

On day 1 the hero showed Reviews 0 and New 0 next to Turns 10, so it read as empty.

  • Plan tiles show only the kinds today holds: Reviews, New, Turns and a new kind, Drills (with what they are: “sweep, crossings”).
  • One to three tiles in a row, four as 2 × 2.
  • A zero shows only when it explains something: “New 0 · paused” in a backlog.
  • If a plan were ever truly empty, the hero becomes the Done state; the tab never shows a Start with nothing behind it.

Boards: V31-10, V31-11.

2 · Three counts of what you know

Today said City memory 20, Explore “Streets you know 9 / 4 320”, Me “Streets known 9”, and Explore’s areas used other totals (Vake 3 / 81).

  • City memory is the only count: streets you’d place today, out of the city’s 3 216.
  • Today: the row. Progress: the card and the district rows. Explore: the map fills, the header figure and every area row (“4 of 264 in memory”). Me: “Streets in memory”, City memory summed over your cities.
  • Seen, Known, Solid and Mastered stay as the stages inside it, never as separate totals.

Boards: V31-10, V31-20, V31-30, V31-40.

3 · Headers and settings differ by tab

  • Today, Explore and Progress share one header: the city switcher on the left, the streak pill on the right. On Explore it floats on the map.
  • Me keeps its “Me” title and the gear. Settings opens from Me only; the gear leaves Explore.
  • A city row on Me also switches city and opens Today for it.

Boards: all four.

4 · The City memory card on two tabs

  • Today gets a one-line row (figure, change, thin bar) that opens Progress.
  • Progress owns the card: figure, change, chart, bar, legend and the one-sentence note.
  • A drop shows in secondary text on both, never red.

Boards: V31-10, V31-30.

5 · The chart is thin on day 1

  • A fixed 14-day frame with a point a day for the first two weeks, then a point a week, up to the last 10 weeks.
  • Day 1 is one dot on a 0–100 scale with the 14 days ticked; the scale never tops out under 100.
  • A caption names the window (“Your line starts today …”).

Boards: V31-30. Needs the daily history below.
