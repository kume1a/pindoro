Drills that use the city’s structure

  • Family sweep appears when 3 or more children of one family are due within 2 days; each placed label grades that child’s pin card.
  • Crossings: one drill a day on a Solid parent or grid street; typed names fuzzy-match (normalised Levenshtein ≤ 0.25). Errors become confusion pairs. Not scheduled itself.
