Changes MemoryChart

City memory chart: windows

Rule

  • Days 1–14: a fixed 14-day frame, a point a day, empty days ticked on the base line.
  • From day 15: a point a week from day 1. From week 10: the last 10 weeks.
  • The scale starts at 0 and never tops out under 100, so day 1’s 20 sits low instead of filling the chart.
  • Today is the larger dot, ringed in the surface colour. One gridline label per step (50 / 100, 250 / 500 …).
  • A caption under the chart says which window is showing.

Tokens

color.chart.line, color.chart.area, color.line.base (grid), color.line.strong (base line).
