The route skeleton and your landmarks

  • Gateways (G): about 6 per neighbourhood, unlocked when 70 % of its parent streets are Known. Graded like a pin plus the street name.
  • Bridge access (B): about 48 items (12 bridges × 2 banks × about 2 approaches), right after the skeleton streets.
  • Personal landmarks: long-press a junction or use “here” while parked; name, type, snapped junction and approach. Salience 1.0, so they win over OSM landmarks on junction cards.
  • Landmark cards: L1 landmark → pin, L2 landmark → junction, L3 junction + turn → landmark.

Flutter: Landmark {id, name, kind, source: osm|personal, junctionId, approachEdgeId, side, salience}. OSM salience formula in the rules board.
