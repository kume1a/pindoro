Progress that reflects real memory

  • Known = a successful pin review on a later day than its intro and R ≥ 0.80 now. Solid = S ≥ 21 d. Mastered = S ≥ 90 d, last review a success, plus name and extent cards (where they apply) at S ≥ 21 d.
  • Recall rate = share of the last 30 days’ due reviews passed; it should sit near 0.90. Below 0.85 means grading or weights need tuning.
  • Whole-city date = remaining core streets ÷ current new-per-day.
  • Settings: minutes a day (sets new streets: 7 / 10 / 14), home and work anchors, which practice types are on, voice model, plate script, reminder.
