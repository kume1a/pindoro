Landmark salience (OSM)

salience = 0.30 type + 0.15 brand or name + 0.15 size + 0.10 wiki + 0.15 uniqueness + 0.15 structural

  • Type scores: metro 1.0, bridge 1.0, square or named roundabout 0.9, fuel 0.9, worship 0.8, mall 0.8, transport hub 0.8, hospital or university 0.7, monument 0.7, stadium or park 0.6, bank, pharmacy, fast food 0.4, signals 0.3
  • Structural: 1.0 within 40 m on the approach-side corner of a turn, 0.6 far corner, 0.3 along the route
  • Personal landmarks: 1.0 and always preferred on their junction
