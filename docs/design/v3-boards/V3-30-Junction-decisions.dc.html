Junction cards (J)

  • Cue: the street you’re on, the heading, the destination and the landmark at the corner. Answer: one of the junction’s legal exits (2–5 buttons on the arms), plus the street you turn onto for full credit.
  • Local map is unlabelled and zoomed to the junction; your approach is a cobalt arrow; the correct exit is yellow.
  • Exit right but street wrong → Hard. Wrong exit → Again and re-asked this session; the sibling junction it’s confused with is named.

Flutter: Junction item = (junctionId, approachEdgeId, exitEdgeId) on the directed, turn-restricted graph. New icons: turn-left, straight, turn-right, fuel-station.
