First meeting with a street

  • New streets come in groups of 3 from the same neighbourhood, mixed into reviews: intro → retrieval 1 after 3–6 other items (district zoom) → retrieval 2 at the end of the session (city zoom).

  • Intro facts: length, type, crossings, direction and distance from the centre, and name:etymology when OSM has it. TTS reads the name in the city’s language.
  • Children (“Nth Lane / Dead End of X”) never come before their parent reaches S ≥ 3 d; at most 4 siblings a day.
  • Guess first: shown for children whose parent is known. Graded, not scheduled.
