Changed in v3

Today replaces the Path and Free play home

  • Tabs: Today · Explore · Progress · Me. The Path, the Pit stop tab, XP, levels and leagues are gone.
  • One button starts today’s session. Its size comes from the time setting (15 / 20 / 30 min): reviews due first, then up to N new streets (7 / 10 / 14).
  • City memory = Σ retrievability over the 3 282 core streets. It goes down when you stop; the delta is shown against yesterday.
  • Streak counts a day only when the due queue is cleared or the day’s minutes are spent. Freezes stay.
  • Backlog rule: if due × 12 s > budget, new streets are 0 and the session takes the most overdue first.
  • “Drive this run today” picks a run whose junction cards are due; it opens the GPS-free drive.

Flutter: TodayController reads SchedulerService.dueCount(now), planSession(budgetMin) and MemoryStats.cityMemory(). New icon: chart (Progress tab). StatefulShellRoute with 4 branches; Explore is the MVP Home minus XP.
