Placement (about 15 min, once per city)

  • Asks all 88 skeleton streets, then 5 grid streets per district; if 4 of 5 pass, sweeps the rest of that district’s grid.
  • A first-sight Good or Easy creates the card as FSRS Good/Easy (S0 = 2.31 d / 8.30 d). “I don’t know this one” leaves it unseen, with no penalty.
  • Ends by naming where new streets start (home anchor chunk) and how many went to review.
